About the Game

Fight against four AI opponents to get the highest K/D in a futuristic training simulator arena. Either use your pistol with infinite ammo or your rifle with limited ammo. Standing still gives more accuracy but makes you an easier target. Kill the AI as much as possible while trying to die as little as possible. The AI won't go down easy and will run and try to heal themselves if they get too low on health.

About the Project

We had to make a first person shooter game set in an arena where the player must fight against enemy AI.

Requirements:

  • The game had to have an arena modelled by us
  • The player and enemy AI had to be able to: walk forwards, walk backwards, strafe left, strafe right, jump, sprint and switch between weapons
  • The player and enemy AI had to have 100 health
  • The player and enemy AI had to have a default firearm with infinite ammunition that does 3 damage
  • The player and enemy AI had to be able to receive a firearm that has limited ammunition through a pickup that does 12 damage
  • There had to be a health pickup that healed 25 health
  • The enemy AI had to patrol and behave similar to a player
  • There had to be a respawn system
  • There had to be a leaderboard
  • We had to use SOLID principles while programming
  • We had to use the event observer pattern, state pattern and one other design pattern

My Contribution

I was the sole programmer for this project. I handled all the C++ and blueprint programming. I did the behavior trees and animation blueprints as well.
