About the Game

Seeds of Change is a farming simulator where the choices you make affect your survival and your community's.

In a small village facing a food crisis, you take on the role of a farmer caught between two obligations, feeding your family or feeding your community. Till the soil, plant seeds, and keep your farm alive through changing seasons, unpredictable weather, and the ever-present threats of drought and pests. But every harvest comes up with the same question, “Do you feed your family or your community?” Neither answer comes without a cost.

About the Project

We had to design and create a game based on one of the United Nations' Stable Development Goals. We chose to do one based on SDG number 2: Zero Hunger.

Requirements:

  • The game had to be based on a UN SDG
  • The game had to bring awarness to the SDG issue
  • The game had to incorporate elements from research
  • The game had to have stakes
  • The game had to provide a challenge
  • The game had to have an obstacle the player must overcome
  • The game had to have a win and lose condition

My Contribution

I was the sole programmer for this project. I handled all the coding in C# and also designed the level and added the code to assets to make them functional.
