About the Game

In this tower defence game the map is procedurally generated every time you start the game. Three winding paths lead towards your main tower which enemies will follow. Place defenders in spots along the path to defend your tower.

Defeating enemies and waves earns you money that can be used to spend to heal your main tower, buy defenders or upgrade defenders.

The amount of enemies and how strong they are is determined by how well the player plays. A procedurally generated rock golem enemy also starts spawning from wave 15 onwards.

About the Project

I had to make a procedurally generated tower defence game with enemies that follow the path to the main tower. The player needs to defend that tower with their own defenders and enemies get harder/easier based on how well/badly the player plays.

Requirements:

  • Procedurally generated level and at least 3 procedurally generated paths leading to the main tower
  • At least 3 defenders for the player to be able to place
  • At least 3 attackers to attack the tower
  • Difficulty that varies based on how well/badly the player plays
  • At least 2 upgrades per defender
  • 1 vertex displacement shader and 1 colour changing shader
  • 1 particle effect and 1 full screen effect
  • 1 additional procedurally generated feature

My Contribution

I was the sole contributor to this project. I did all the C++ programming, shader programming, animation programming myself. I used 1 sword that I made in 2025 and the remaining assets I used from free online sources. I used C++ as much as possible, the only times I used blueprints was for animation and when it's required to assign the input to the player controller.
